Assets

Upload project images into the assets/ folder in the site root. Recommended filenames are listed in assets/README.md. For production, upload optimized WebP or AVIF images.

If your browser supports folder access, choose the project root and the image will be saved into assets/images. Otherwise it will just populate the path field for manual upload.
