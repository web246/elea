About Elea

Founded on care and detail.

Elea was founded by Joan Kayaga in Berlin with a simple conviction: that a clean, organized home is the foundation of a calmer, more intentional life.

What began as a commitment to meticulous, respectful home care has grown into a full-service home organization brand — one that treats every home as a space worthy of genuine attention. From routine cleaning to complete wardrobe transformations, Elea approaches each visit with the same editorial standard: nothing overlooked, nothing rushed.

We believe home care is not a transaction but a relationship. We learn your space, respect your routines, and return with consistency — so your home always feels cared for, never just cleaned.
